Before going real
  1. Practice here until you have 50+ trades with a profit factor above 1.0 and a drawdown you can live with.
  2. Choose a broker licensed by your country’s regulator (in Kenya, check the Capital Markets Authority list) and use their demo account first.
  3. Start with a small deposit and risk 1% or less per trade, always with a stop loss.
  4. Only use money you can afford to lose. Most retail forex traders lose money.

Learn: the basics (tap to open)

Forex: you trade one currency against another. Buying EUR/USD bets the euro rises; selling bets it falls.

Candles: each candle shows open, high, low and close for a short period. Green closed higher than it opened, red closed lower. The thin lines are the high and low.

Spread: you buy at the higher ask and sell at the lower bid. That gap is the cost of every trade.

Pips and lots: a pip is 0.0001 (0.01 on JPY pairs). One lot is 100,000 units; on EUR/USD, 1 pip on 0.10 lots is about $1.

Leverage: 1:100 here. It lets a small margin control a big position and magnifies wins and losses equally.

Risk: always set a stop loss and risk only 1 to 2% of the account per trade. Aim for 50+ demo trades before judging a strategy.
